FERAL LAB

Rotation Simulator

A seeded, time-based Monte Carlo model of energy, mana, combo points, procs, bleeds, buffs, attacks, and consumables.

Experimental resource model — not a DPS simulator

Results depend on the selected settings and modeled assumptions, including mechanics that may differ from the live game. More casts or a higher optimizer score do not prove higher damage or a best rotation. Review the assumptions and the notes beside each setting before interpreting or sharing results.

Shifting Power normally grants 40 energy on an 8s cooldown. Howling Idol reduces the cooldown to 7s; Tier 1 Feral 5pc reduces it to 6.9s, or 5.9s with the idol. Wolfshead Helm changes the gain to 60 energy. All three can be enabled together. Mana cost and 1s GCD stay unchanged. Optimization keeps these equipment choices fixed.

Rotation

Finishers require at least their selected CP minimum, then consume all current CP on a landed cast. Rip lasts 12s; damage is not modeled. With less than 12s left in the actual fight, eligible Bite takes priority over Rip/Rake and ignores both Rip-safety timers. Bite's CP minimum and energy limits still apply; Rip remains a fallback if Bite is unavailable. The optimizer can search both CP minimums; uncheck their search toggles to hold them fixed.

Bite tradeoffs

Exhaustive selected grid + current settings at 5 CP + terminal-only control (30/30 safety). Shared seeds and fight lengths, 25,000-fight limit. Lower safety permits more aggressive weaving. At a 35-energy ceiling a non-Clearcast Bite requires exactly 35 energy; 12s safety on a 12s Rip effectively disables ordinary weaving.

Model assumptions

Berserk GCD: enabled by default: Berserk at −1s, first attack at 0s, 14s of combat uptime, cooldown ready at 179s. Disabled: Berserk at 0s with the first attack, 15s uptime, cooldown ready at 180s; later uses are also off GCD. If Faerie Fire is enabled, cast it at −2s with on-GCD Berserk or −1s with off-GCD Berserk. Pre-pull casts count in totals; regeneration, attacks, and measured combat time start at 0s.

Clearcasting: vanilla WoWSims Classic model: 2 PPM, 10s internal cooldown, 15s buff. Landed cat autos roll 3.33%; landed specials use equipped weapon speed × 2 / 60. Default weapon speed is an assumed 3.0s; set yours above. Swing timer only changes autoattack frequency. Rake, Shred, Rip, and Bite consume the buff even on avoidance; landed Bite still drains remaining energy. Shifting Power neither benefits from nor consumes Clearcasting.

Starting Clearcasting: optional, requires Omen of Clarity. Assumes a fresh proc at pull (t=0): 15s buff remaining and no new Omen roll before t=10s, even after the free cast consumes it. Pre-pull Faerie Fire/Berserk do not shorten it. Shifting Power does not consume it. The starting buff is reported separately from rolled procs; its free cast and energy savings are included. Optimization keeps this starting condition fixed.

Natural Shapeshifter: 0/10/20/30% reduction makes Shifting Power cost 684/615.6/547.2/478.8 mana, without integer rounding. Energy gain, cooldown, GCD, and the five-second-rule reset are unchanged.

Reflection: retain 0/17/33/50% of Spirit regeneration inside the five-second rule. Outside it, Spirit regeneration is always 100%.

Windfury: vanilla WoWSims Classic Totem rules, with the feral restriction removed: 20% on landed autos and Rake/Shred/Rip/Bite, 1.5s internal cooldown. One attack advances and resets the swing timer; auto-triggered attacks wait the 10ms batch, special-triggered attacks occur immediately. These white attacks can miss/dodge/parry/crit and proc Omen or Judgment; the ICD prevents Windfury chaining. No GCD, energy, or CP gain. This resource simulator does not calculate damage, so the Totem's bonus AP is not modeled.

Shift timing: automatic mode compares cap loss now (including regeneration during Shift's GCD) against later cap loss + energy gain × delay / cooldown. It considers spending first, Clearcasting, known Tea, and gear bonuses; ties favor shifting now. Imminent cooldowns use the actual delay caused by another GCD. Forecasts assume landed attacks and only guaranteed bonus CP, without predicting new random procs. This is a local energy-loss heuristic, not a guarantee of maximum CPM. Manual mode restores the fixed threshold. Shift remains highest priority when the selected rule favors it and mana is available. The optimizer holds the mode fixed and skips threshold search in automatic mode.

Shred & Tea: Shred's 4s refresh lookahead counts regeneration during the GCD, reachable off-GCD Tea, and Shifting Power's mana, selected timing mode, cooldown, and full GCD. It checks Rake/Rip together and only pools if Shred would delay a protected refresh compared with waiting. Tea is checked immediately after spending energy as well as between GCDs; the selected ≤10-energy policy still applies.

Faerie Fire: lowest-priority filler, only when a full 1s GCD will not delay a known higher-priority action. Forecasts include scheduled energy/mana ticks, cooldowns, bleed and buff timing, and usable Tea. Future random procs are not predicted; any affordable Shred is conservatively protected even while pooling for a refresh.

Model note: failed finishers retain combo points and spend their base energy cost unless Clearcasting is active. OOM means the first time Shifting Power is due by policy but its current mana cost is unavailable.
